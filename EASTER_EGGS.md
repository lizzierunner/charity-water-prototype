# 🥚 H2O Hero Quiz Easter Eggs

## Keyboard Shortcuts
- Press 'Escape' to reset quiz (with confirmation)
- Press '1-4' to select answers
- Press 'Enter' to submit answer
- Press 'C' to trigger confetti (on results page)

## Console Messages
- ASCII art H2O Hero logo
- "Found the console! You're a true hero 💧"
- Hidden cheat codes info
- charity: water mission statement

## Special Names
- Enter "charity water" for a special badge
- Enter "Scott Harrison" for founder badge
- Enter "H2O" for a secret message

## Click Counter
- Click logo 10 times to unlock a bonus fact
- Click droplet particles for a small animation

## Konami Code
- Up, Up, Down, Down, Left, Right, Left, Right, B, A
- Triggers mega celebration animation

## Perfect Score Easter Egg
- Special message from the charity: water team
- Bonus legendary animation

A Well for Hope Village

In Hope Village, Ethiopia, access to clean water was once a daily struggle. Women and children walked miles to collect water from unsafe sources, risking their health and safety.

Thanks to a charity: water project, a new well was built in the heart of the community. Now, families have clean, safe water just steps from their homes.

Children spend more time in school, and parents can focus on work and family. The entire village has been transformed by the gift of water.

Since completion, the well has provided over 1,000,000 liters of clean water, improving health and opportunity for all.
